2. Safety Spine

AutoLuno must copy AutoPoly's safety posture before adding more automation: read first, store locally, simulate/paper second, and require explicit guarded review before any live exchange mutation.

Allowed Now

  • Read Luno ticker, balance, fee, trade, and market data.
  • Store historical snapshots in SQLite.
  • Edit local settings for schedulers and bot parameters.
  • Start/stop bot lifecycle as a paper-only acknowledgement.

Blocked Now

  • No market orders from the app.
  • No withdrawals from the app.
  • No autonomous bot execution against real funds.
  • No committed API keys or secrets.
RuleReason
Mutation-capable Luno endpoints stay disabled.Orders and withdrawals affect real funds and require AutoPoly-style guard/risk/audit first.
Scheduler settings are typed singleton records.Each job can be audited separately: fees, markets, and trades.
Runtime credentials stay in untracked env files.Tracked test env files are historical risk and should be removed/rotated.
